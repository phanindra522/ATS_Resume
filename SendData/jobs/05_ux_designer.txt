UX Designer - User Experience

Company: DesignFirst Studio
Location: Los Angeles, CA (Hybrid)
Salary Range: $85,000 - $120,000

We are seeking a creative and user-focused UX Designer to join our design team and create exceptional user experiences for our digital products.

Job Description:
As a UX Designer, you will be responsible for researching user needs, creating wireframes and prototypes, and designing intuitive user interfaces. You will work closely with product managers, developers, and other designers to deliver user-centered solutions.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or related field
- 3-4 years of UX design experience
- Portfolio demonstrating strong UX design skills
- Proficiency in design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe Creative Suite)
- Experience with user research and usability testing
- Understanding of design systems and accessibility
- Strong communication and collaboration skills
- Knowledge of front-end development principles

Skills:
- User Research
- Wireframing
- Prototyping
- Figma
- Sketch
- Adobe Creative Suite
- Usability Testing
- Information Architecture
- Interaction Design
- Visual Design
- Design Systems
- Accessibility
- User Journey Mapping
- A/B Testing

Responsibilities:
- Conduct user research and analyze user behavior
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
- Design intuitive and accessible user interfaces
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Conduct usability testing and iterate on designs
- Maintain and evolve design systems
- Present design concepts to stakeholders
- Stay updated with UX design trends and best practices

Join our creative team and help shape user experiences that make a difference.
