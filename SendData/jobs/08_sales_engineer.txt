Sales Engineer - Technical Sales

Company: TechSolutions Pro
Location: Boston, MA (Travel Required)

- Excellent presentation and communication skills
- Experience with CRM systems and sales processes
- Ability to travel to customer sites (up to 50% travel)
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
- Customer-focused mindset

Skills:
- Technical Sales

- Customer Presentations
- CRM Systems
- Technical Support
- Solution Architecture
- Customer Relationship Management
- Negotiation
- Market Analysis

- Develop technical proposals and solution architectures
- Build relationships with technical decision makers
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Provide feedback on customer requirements and market trends
- Achieve sales targets and revenue goals

This role offers the opportunity to combine technical expertise with sales skills in a growing technology company.
