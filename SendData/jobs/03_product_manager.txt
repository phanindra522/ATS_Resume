Product Manager - Digital Products

Company: InnovateTech Inc.
Location: Seattle, WA (Hybrid)
Salary Range: $110,000 - $150,000

We are seeking a strategic Product Manager to lead the development of our digital products and drive product strategy across multiple platforms.

Job Description:
As a Product Manager, you will be responsible for defining product vision, creating roadmaps, and working closely with engineering and design teams to deliver exceptional user experiences. You will analyze market trends, gather user feedback, and make data-driven decisions to improve our products.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, or related field
- 4-6 years of product management experience
- Experience with agile development methodologies
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and leadership abilities
- Experience with product analytics tools
- Understanding of user experience principles
- Technical background preferred

Skills:
- Product Strategy
- Agile Development
- User Research
- Data Analysis
- Market Research
- Project Management
- Stakeholder Management
- Product Analytics
- A/B Testing
- User Experience Design
- Business Analysis
- Leadership

Responsibilities:
- Define product vision and strategy for digital products
- Create and maintain product roadmaps
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and marketing teams
- Conduct user research and analyze user feedback
- Define product requirements and user stories
- Monitor product performance and key metrics
- Coordinate product launches and go-to-market strategies
- Present product updates to executive leadership

Join our team and help shape the future of digital products in a fast-paced, innovative environment.
