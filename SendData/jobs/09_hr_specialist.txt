HR Specialist - Talent Acquisition

Company: PeopleFirst Corp
Location: Denver, CO (Hybrid)
Salary Range: $60,000 - $85,000

We are seeking a dedicated HR Specialist to join our human resources team and focus on talent acquisition and employee relations.

Job Description:
As an HR Specialist, you will be responsible for recruiting top talent, managing the hiring process, and supporting various HR functions. You will work closely with hiring managers to identify staffing needs and ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the recruitment process.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business, or related field
- 2-4 years of HR or recruiting experience
- Knowledge of employment laws and regulations
- Experience with applicant tracking systems (ATS)
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information
- Organizational and time management skills
- HR certification preferred (PHR, SHRM-CP)

Skills:
- Talent Acquisition
- Recruitment
- Interviewing
- Applicant Tracking Systems
- Employee Relations
- HR Policies
- Onboarding
- Performance Management
- Benefits Administration
- Compliance
- Communication
- Organization
- Confidentiality
- Problem Solving

Responsibilities:
- Source and recruit candidates for open positions
- Screen resumes and conduct initial interviews
- Coordinate with hiring managers throughout the recruitment process
- Manage job postings and recruitment channels
- Conduct reference checks and background verifications
- Assist with new employee onboarding
- Maintain employee records and HR databases
- Support employee relations and HR policy implementation

Join our HR team and help us build a strong, diverse workforce in a supportive company culture.
